Active-Passive Architecture

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

One site serves production traffic while a standby site is kept ready to take over on failure.

Simplify consistency by keeping a single writer while maintaining a replicated warm or cold standby for recovery.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

Availability and disaster recovery without the complexity of multi-writer data.

It is simpler, cheaper to reason about, and often adequate for RTO/RPO targets.

Traffic goes to the primary; the standby takes over on failover

How it works

Users are routed only to the primary site.
Data replicates continuously to the standby.
Health checks monitor the primary.
On failure, the standby replica is promoted.
DNS or load balancer switches traffic to the standby.
After recovery, the primary is rebuilt and failback occurs.
