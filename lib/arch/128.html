MAJOR SERVICES & COMPONENTS
COMMUNICATION MECHANISMS

Accounts, Payments orchestrator, Ledger, Fraud, AML/compliance, Limits, Notifications, Reconciliation, Identity.

Synchronous calls for authorization and fraud scoring; Kafka for payment events, audit and reconciliation; idempotency keys on every payment request.

DATA STORAGE ARCHITECTURE
EXTERNAL DEPENDENCIES

Double-entry ledger as append-only events (event sourcing); relational store for accounts; WORM archive for audit; warehouse for analytics.

Payment networks (UPI, card, SWIFT), core banking, KYC providers, credit bureaus, HSM/KMS.

CHOSEN ARCHITECTURAL STYLE
WHY THIS ARCHITECTURE WAS SELECTED

Event-sourced ledger with orchestrated sagas, hexagonal services and active-passive multi-region DR.

The ledger must be auditable and never lose or doublepost a transaction, so immutable events and idempotent commands are preferred. Orchestration gives payments a visible, regulated state machine.

KEY TRADE-OFFS

Higher engineering and operational cost; strong consistency limits throughput per account; active-passive DR accepts minutes of RTO for simpler data correctness.
