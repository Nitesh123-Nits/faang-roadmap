III.02

Publish–Subscribe Architecture

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

Publishers send messages to topics without knowing subscribers; subscribers receive messages for topics they have subscribed to.

A broker mediates delivery so producers and consumers are decoupled in time, space and quantity.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

Point-to-point integration where each producer must know and call every consumer.

Real-time feeds and large integration landscapes needed one-to-many distribution without coupling.

A topic fans out one message to many subscribers

How it works

A publisher sends a message to a named topic.
The broker stores or routes it to all current subscriptions.
Each subscriber receives its own copy.
Subscribers acknowledge; the broker tracks delivery per subscription.
New subscribers can join without touching the publisher.
Failures are retried or sent to a dead-letter topic.
