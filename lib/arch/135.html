MAJOR SERVICES & COMPONENTS
COMMUNICATION MECHANISMS

Gateway and tenant router, Identity/SSO, Core product services, Integration and webhook service, Metering and billing, Admin/provisioning, Audit logging.

REST APIs and webhooks for integrations; events for metering and audit; tenant context propagated in tokens and headers.

DATA STORAGE ARCHITECTURE
EXTERNAL DEPENDENCIES

Pooled database with row-level security for small tenants; schema- or database-per-tenant for larger ones; per-tenant encryption keys; object storage with tenant prefixes; audit log store.

Customer IdPs (SAML/OIDC), CRM/ERP integrations, billing and tax providers, email services.

CHOSEN ARCHITECTURAL STYLE
WHY THIS ARCHITECTURE WAS SELECTED

Multi-tenant microservices or modular monolith deployed in cells, with tiered tenant isolation and eventdriven metering.

Pooling small tenants keeps cost low while dedicated cells meet enterprise isolation and compliance demands; cells contain blast radius and allow staged rollouts.

KEY TRADE-OFFS

Noisy-neighbor and isolation risks; operational complexity of multiple tiers; per-tenant customization pressure; cell routing and migration tooling.
