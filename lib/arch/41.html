III.13

Blackboard Architecture

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

Independent specialist components (knowledge sources) cooperate by reading and writing a shared data structure (the blackboard), coordinated by a controller.

When no deterministic algorithm exists, let specialists incrementally refine partial solutions on a shared space.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

Problems with uncertain, incremental solutions such as speech recognition or signal interpretation.

Originated in the Hearsay-II speech recognition system (1970s) for non-deterministic problem solving.

Knowledge sources contribute to a shared blackboard under a controller

How it works

Initial data is placed on the blackboard.
The controller watches the blackboard for changes.
It selects and activates the knowledge source best suited to progress.
The knowledge source reads partial results and posts new hypotheses.
Iteration continues until a satisfactory solution emerges.
The controller decides termination.
