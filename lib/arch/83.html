VI.08

Medallion Architecture (Bronze, Silver, Gold)

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

A layered lakehouse design that progressively refines data through Bronze (raw), Silver (cleansed) and Gold (business-ready) tables.

Improve data quality in stages so each layer has a clear contract and consumers choose the right level.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

Unstructured pipelines where raw and business-ready data are mixed, making quality and lineage unclear.

Popularized by Databricks as a clean pattern for Delta Lake pipelines.

How it works

Raw data lands unchanged in Bronze with metadata (source, load time).
Silver jobs clean, deduplicate, validate and conform schemas.
Gold jobs aggregate and model data for business use cases.
Each layer is a set of versioned, ACID tables.
Quality checks gate promotion between layers.
Consumers read from Silver or Gold as appropriate.
MEDALLION ARCHITECTURE (BRONZE, SILVER, GOLD)

Disadvantages
