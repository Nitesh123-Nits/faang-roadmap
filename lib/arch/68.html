Event-Driven Microservices

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

Microservices that collaborate through events on a broker, each reacting to facts and keeping local read models.

Combine service autonomy with asynchronous event flow for loose coupling and resilience.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

Synchronous call chains that couple availability and latency across services.

Event backbones such as Kafka made reliable asynchronous integration practical at scale.

How it works

A service commits state and an outbox record in one local transaction.
A relay (CDC) publishes the record to Kafka.
Subscribers consume the event at their own pace.
Each updates its local data or triggers new events.
Failures use retries, DLQs and idempotent handlers.
Event schemas are versioned in a registry.
