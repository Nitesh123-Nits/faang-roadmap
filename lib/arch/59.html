Service Mesh Architecture

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

A dedicated infrastructure layer of sidecar proxies that handles service-to-service communication — mTLS, retries, routing and telemetry.

Move networking concerns out of application code into a transparent proxy layer managed by a central control plane.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

Duplicated resilience, security and observability logic across many services and languages.

Polyglot microservice fleets needed uniform eastwest traffic control (Istio, Linkerd, Envoy).

Data plane sidecars controlled by a control plane

How it works

Each service pod runs with a sidecar proxy.
All inbound and outbound traffic passes through the proxy.
The control plane pushes routing rules, policies and certificates.
Proxies establish mTLS and apply retries, timeouts and circuit breaking.
Traffic shifting enables canary and blue/green releases.
Proxies emit uniform metrics, logs and traces.
