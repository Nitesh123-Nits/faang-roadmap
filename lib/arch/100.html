Disaster Recovery Architecture

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

Planned capability to restore systems and data after a major outage, defined by RPO (data loss tolerance) and RTO (recovery time).

Choose a recovery strategy matching business tolerance, from backup and restore to warm standby and multi-site active-active.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

Loss of an entire site or region, data corruption and ransomware.

Regulations and business continuity plans require tested recovery objectives.

DR strategies from cheap and slow to costly and fast
