Data Lake vs Data Warehouse vs Lakehouse

These differ in what is stored, when structure is imposed and how reliably the data can be queried. Lakehouse combines lake storage economics with warehouse reliability.

Complexity trade-offs

Suitable use cases

A lake is cheap and flexible but needs governance work; a warehouse is reliable but rigid and costly; a lakehouse shifts complexity into the table format and catalog while removing duplicated pipelines.

Lake: raw archive, ML experimentation.
Warehouse: governed enterprise reporting.
Lakehouse: unified BI, ML and streaming on one platform.

Practical recommendation. For new platforms choose a lakehouse with medallion layers. Keep a managed warehouse where BI performance and simplicity dominate and data is mostly structured.
