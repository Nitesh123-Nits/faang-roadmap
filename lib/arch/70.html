Cell-Based Architecture

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

The system is split into independent, identical cells, each a complete stack serving a subset of users or tenants, behind a thin routing layer.

Limit blast radius: a failure or bad deployment affects only one cell’s share of users.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

Large-scale outages caused by shared components and cascading failures.

Used at AWS and Slack to contain failures and scale by adding cells rather than growing shared services.

A router assigns users to isolated cells

How it works

A router maps each user or tenant to a cell.
Requests go to the assigned cell and are served entirely within it.
Cells share nothing at runtime, including data.
A failure or bad release is contained to one cell.
New capacity is added by creating new cells.
Releases roll out cell by cell.
