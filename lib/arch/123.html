Active-Active vs Active-Passive

Both provide availability across sites. Active-active serves traffic from all sites simultaneously; active-passive serves from one and keeps another ready.

Complexity trade-offs

Suitable use cases

Active-active shifts complexity into data conflict handling and capacity planning; active-passive keeps data simple but depends on failover procedures that must be rehearsed.

Active-active: always-on global systems with partitionable or conflict-tolerant data.
Active-passive: strongly consistent systems with moderate RTO/RPO.

Practical recommendation. Use active-passive unless the availability or latency target truly requires activeactive. If you go active-active, partition data by home region and automate failover tests.
