CQRS vs Event Sourcing

Complexity trade-offs

Suitable use cases

CQRS adds a synchronization problem; Event Sourcing adds a modeling and schema-evolution problem. Together they are the most complex combination; adopt each only for its own benefit.

CQRS only: read-heavy domains with complex queries.
Event Sourcing only: audit-critical domains (ledgers).
Both: regulated, history-rich domains with multiple read views.

Practical recommendation. Adopt CQRS alone first when reads and writes diverge. Add Event Sourcing only where history, audit or replay is a genuine business requirement.
