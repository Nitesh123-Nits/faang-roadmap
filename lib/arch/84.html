Data Pipeline Architecture

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

An automated sequence of stages that ingests, transforms, validates and delivers data between systems, in batch or streaming mode.

Treat data movement as software: orchestrated, tested, observable and idempotent.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

Manual, brittle data transfers and inconsistent analytics inputs.

Growing numbers of sources and consumers required reliable, repeatable data delivery.

Ingest, process, store, serve with orchestration and monitoring

How it works

The orchestrator triggers or monitors each stage.
Ingestion pulls or receives data from sources.
Transformations clean, join and enrich the data.
Results are loaded to the target stores.
Quality checks validate outputs and alert on anomalies.
Failed stages retry or rerun idempotently with lineage tracking.
