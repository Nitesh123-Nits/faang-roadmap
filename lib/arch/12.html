Event-Driven Architecture

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

Components communicate by producing and reacting to events — immutable facts that something happened.

Decouple producers from consumers through an event channel so each reacts independently and asynchronously.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

Tight temporal and structural coupling of request/response calls between components.

Systems needed real-time reactions, loose coupling and elastic scaling beyond what synchronous calls offered.

Producers publish events to a broker; consumers react independently

How it works

Something happens in a service (OrderPlaced).
The service publishes an immutable event to a broker topic.
The broker stores and delivers it to all interested consumers.
Consumers process independently at their own pace.
Consumers may emit new events, creating reaction chains.
Failed processing is retried or routed to a dead-letter queue.
