III.03

Message-Driven Architecture

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

Components communicate through asynchronous messages, often targeted commands or requests, delivered via queues with a specific recipient.

Messages are addressed work or data items; the sender expects some component to handle them, decoupling timing and failure.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

Blocking calls and availability coupling between components.

Enterprise messaging (JMS, AMQP) enabled reliable asynchronous work distribution.

Producer sends commands to a queue; competing consumers process them

How it works

A sender creates a message addressed to a queue or endpoint.
The broker persists it until a consumer is available.
One consumer from a pool receives and processes it.
The consumer acknowledges success; failures are redelivered.
Poison messages move to a dead-letter queue.
Results may flow back through a reply queue or a follow-up message.
