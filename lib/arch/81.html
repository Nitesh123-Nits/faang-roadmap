Data Mesh Architecture

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

A decentralized approach where domain teams own and publish data as products, supported by a selfserve platform and federated governance.

Treat data as a product owned by domains rather than a centralized team’s pipeline output.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

Central data team bottlenecks and lack of domain context in large organizations.

Zhamak Dehghani proposed it (2019) as data volumes and domains outgrew centralized lakes.

How it works

Domains own their operational and analytical data.
Each domain publishes data products with contracts and SLAs.
A self-serve platform provides tooling to build and run them.
Products are discoverable through a catalog.
Federated governance sets global standards and policies.
Consumers combine products across domains.
