Orchestration vs Choreography

Both coordinate multi-service processes. Orchestration puts the process logic in one controller; choreography distributes it across services reacting to events.

Advantages & disadvantages

OPTIONADVANTAGESDISADVANTAGES
OrchestrationClear flow; easy compensation, timeouts, monitoringOrchestrator can become a god service or bottleneck
ChoreographyLoose coupling; no central bottleneck; easy to add reactionsHidden flow; cyclic dependencies; hard to debug

Complexity trade-offs

Suitable use cases

Orchestration concentrates complexity in one component that needs high availability; choreography spreads it, making the whole harder to see. The crossover is around three or four steps or any branching.

Orchestration: long, branching or regulated processes with SLAs and human steps.
Choreography: short flows with few participants and autonomous teams.

Practical recommendation. Prefer choreography for simple, short event chains. Use orchestration (durable workflow engines) when the process has branching, compensation and visibility needs. Keep domain decisions in services in both cases.
