Multi-Region Architecture

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

An application deployed in multiple geographic regions for lower latency, higher availability and data residency compliance.

Place services and data near users and survive the loss of an entire region.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

Latency for global users, regional outages, and regulatory data residency.

Global products and critical systems cannot rely on a single region.

Global traffic routing across regions with cross-region replication

How it works

DNS or global load balancing routes users to the nearest healthy region.
Each region runs a full application stack.
Data is replicated across regions asynchronously or synchronously.
Health checks detect regional failure.
Traffic fails over to another region.
Writes follow a regional-primary or multi-primary strategy.
