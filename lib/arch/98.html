VII.10

High Availability Architecture

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

Eliminating single points of failure through redundancy and automatic failover so the system meets an availability target (for example 99.99%).

Replicate every critical component across failure domains and detect and route around failures automatically.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

Downtime caused by component, host or zone failures.

Business-critical services have strict uptime SLAs and revenue impact per minute of outage.

How it works

Traffic enters through redundant, health-checked load balancers.
Stateless instances run in at least two or three availability zones.
Data stores are replicated with automatic failover.
Health checks remove failed instances from rotation.
A standby is promoted when the primary fails.
Capacity headroom ensures survivors can carry the load.
