Leader–Follower Architecture

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

A cluster elects one leader to coordinate decisions or serialize writes; followers replicate the leader’s state and take over if it fails.

Use consensus-based election so the group always agrees on a single leader, even across failures.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

Coordination, ordering and automatic failover in distributed clusters without manual promotion.

Consensus algorithms (Paxos, Raft) made leader election safe and standard in systems like etcd, Kafka and ZooKeeper.

Raft-style leader replicates a log to followers by quorum

How it works

Nodes elect a leader through a majority vote.
Clients send writes to the leader.
The leader appends entries and replicates them to followers.
An entry commits once a quorum acknowledges it.
Heartbeats prove the leader is alive.
If the leader fails, followers time out and elect a new leader.
