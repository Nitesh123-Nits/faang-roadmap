API Composition / Aggregator Architecture

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

A composer service queries multiple services and combines their results into one response; the Aggregator pattern is the same idea applied at the service level.

Implement cross-service queries by calling owners and joining in memory instead of joining in the database.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

Queries spanning data owned by several services under database-per-service.

Without shared databases, joins move into a composition layer.

Composer fans out to services and merges results

Service / component

Sync call

How it works

The client asks the composer for a composite view.
The composer calls the owning services, in parallel where possible.
It applies timeouts, retries and fallbacks per call.
It joins and filters the results in memory.
It returns the composed response.
Partial failures yield degraded or cached responses.
