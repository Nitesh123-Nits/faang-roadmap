MVVM-C (MVVM with Coordinators)

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

MVVM extended with Coordinators that own navigation and flow between screens, so ViewModels and views do not know about each other.

Extract navigation responsibility into coordinator objects that create and present screens.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

View controllers or screens that hard-code navigation and become coupled to their neighbors.

Popularized in iOS (Soroush Khanlou) to decouple navigation from view controllers.

Coordinator creates screens and handles flow
Domain core
Service / component

How it works

The app starts a coordinator that builds the first screen.
The coordinator creates the view and its ViewModel and injects dependencies.
The view binds to the ViewModel as in MVVM.
On a navigation event, the ViewModel signals the coordinator.
The coordinator creates and presents the next screen.
Child coordinators manage sub-flows and return control on finish.

Key engineering considerations

Keep coordinators free of business logic. Use delegation or events for results. Test flows by driving coordinators with fake screens.
