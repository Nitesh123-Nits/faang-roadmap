Active-Active Architecture

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

Two or more sites serve production traffic simultaneously, each capable of taking over the others’ load.

Use all capacity all the time and remove failover delay by distributing live traffic across sites.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

Idle standby capacity and slow failover in activepassive designs.

Always-on services need near-zero downtime and efficient resource use.

Both regions serve traffic with bidirectional data replication

How it works

A global load balancer splits traffic across regions.
Each region reads and writes its local data store.
Changes replicate between regions in both directions.
Conflicts are resolved by policy (last-write-wins, CRDTs, partitioning).
If a region fails, the load balancer shifts all traffic to survivors.
Surviving regions must have capacity headroom.
