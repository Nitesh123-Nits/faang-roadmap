MVC vs MVP vs MVVM vs MVI

All separate presentation from data. They differ in who owns presentation logic and how state flows to the view: controller-driven, presenter-driven, binding-driven or unidirectional reducerdriven.

Complexity trade-offs

Suitable use cases

Complexity rises from MVC to MVI as flow becomes more explicit. Explicitness buys predictability and testability for stateful UIs and costs ceremony on simple screens.

MVC: server-rendered web.
MVP: testable UIs without data binding.
MVVM: rich bound UIs on supporting frameworks.
MVI: complex, state-heavy interactive UIs.

Practical recommendation. Follow the platform default (MVC on the server, MVVM on frameworks with binding, MVI-style unidirectional state for complex client state). Do not mix patterns within one feature.
