Distributed Monolith Architecture

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

A system deployed as many services that are so tightly coupled they must be changed, released and scaled together.

Not an intended design — it emerges when service boundaries ignore domain boundaries and coupling is kept.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

Nothing; it is the failure mode that combines the cost of distribution with the coupling of a monolith.

It appears when monoliths are split by technical layer or without ownership of data, or when services share a database.

How it works

A feature change spans services A, B and C.
All three must be released together to stay compatible.
Synchronous call chains make each request depend on all services.
A shared database makes schema changes global.
Failures cascade through the chain.
Latency multiplies; independent scaling is impossible in practice.
