III.09

Choreography-Based Architecture

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

Services coordinate a process by reacting to each other’s events, with no central controller.

Each service knows what to do when it sees certain events, and emits events of its own.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

Central coordinators becoming bottlenecks and coupling points in event-driven systems.

Event-driven microservices naturally lend themselves to decentralized coordination.

Service / component

How it works

The Order service publishes OrderCreated.
Payment reacts, charges and publishes PaymentCompleted.
Inventory reacts, reserves stock and publishes StockReserved.
Shipping reacts and starts fulfillment.
On failures services publish compensating events.
No component owns the full process view.
CHOREOGRAPHY-BASED ARCHITECTURE

Advantages

Disadvantages

Loose coupling and no central bottleneck.
Process logic is spread across services.

+ Easy to add new participants by subscribing.

Hard to see, test and debug end-to-end.
Services remain autonomous.
Risk of cyclic dependencies between events.
Simple for short flows.
Compensation and timeouts are hard to track.

When to use

When not to use

Simple, short event chains with few participants and high autonomy needs.

Complex flows with many steps, branching or strict visibility requirements — prefer orchestration.
