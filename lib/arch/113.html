SOA vs Microservices

Both decompose systems into services. SOA optimizes enterprise-wide reuse and integration through shared middleware; microservices optimize independent delivery with smart endpoints and dumb pipes.

Complexity trade-offs

Suitable use cases

SOA centralizes complexity in the bus and governance; microservices distribute it across services and infrastructure. SOA changes slowly but integrates diverse systems; microservices change fast but demand platform maturity.

SOA: large enterprises integrating many legacy systems under strong governance.
Microservices: product-oriented teams delivering independently on cloud-native platforms.

Practical recommendation. For new product development choose microservices (or a modular monolith first). Keep SOA-style integration only at the boundary with legacy estates, and avoid logic in the bus.
