Federated Architecture

WHAT IS IT?
FUNDAMENTAL IDEA

Autonomous systems or domains cooperate under shared standards and a thin federation layer, retaining independent ownership.

Provide a unified experience or governance across independent subsystems without centralizing them.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
WHY IT WAS INTRODUCED

Integrating separately owned systems (business units, organizations) while preserving autonomy.

Large organizations and ecosystems cannot centralize everything; GraphQL federation and federated identity address this.

How it works

Each domain owns its service, data and schema.
Domains publish their contracts to a registry.
A federation layer composes them into a unified API or identity.
Requests are planned and routed across domains.
Shared standards (auth, naming, SLOs) ensure interoperability.
Domains evolve independently within agreed contracts.
