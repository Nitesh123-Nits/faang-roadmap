Deadlock: circular wait on two locks

Prevent: global lock ordering · tryLock(timeout) · fewer, coarser locks · no locks held while calling foreign code/I/O. Detect: jstack / jcmd Thread.print prints "Found one

Circular wait. Two threads each hold one lock and wait for the other. All four Coffman conditions — mutual exclusion, hold‐and‐wait, no preemption, circular wait — must hold.
