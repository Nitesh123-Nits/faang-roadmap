10.5Liveness vs Readiness vs Startup Probes

Probe timeline: startup →readiness

liveness fails

startup fails
after failureThreshold × periodSeconds →container restarted

Rule: liveness = 'is this process wedged?' (local only). Readiness = 'can I serve right now?'. Never put shared dependencies in liveness.

Three probes, three consequences.

Readiness controls traffic, liveness controls restarts, startup protects slow boots.
