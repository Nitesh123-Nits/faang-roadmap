CH 10 · KUBERNETES

Workloads: Pods, Deployments, ReplicaSets, StatefulSets

Kubernetes objects for one Spring Boot service

Deployment →ReplicaSet →Pods. Service selects pods by labels (not by Deployment). Rollback = switch back to the previous ReplicaSet.

One service, many objects. You declare the desired state; controllers reconcile reality towards it.
