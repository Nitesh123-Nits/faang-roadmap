Accuracy policy

Guarantees are separated from typical implementation behaviour. No benchmark numbers are invented: latency figures are orders of magnitude and every sizing formula is a starting point to validate with load tests. Each chapter ends with links to the official documentation used for version‐sensitive claims. Verify defaults against the exact versions you run — they change between releases.
