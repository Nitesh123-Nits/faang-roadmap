5.18Failure Detection and Recovery

Heartbeats + timeouts = suspicion , not certainty. Too short →false positives and flapping; too long →slow recovery. Phi‐

accrual detectors adapt to observed variance.

Use health checks at several levels: process alive (liveness), can serve (readiness), synthetic end‐to‐end checks.
Recovery must be idempotent and resumable: replay from logs/offsets, reconcile, then resume.
Practice it: game days, chaos experiments, failover drills; measure RTO (time to restore) and RPO (data you may lose).
