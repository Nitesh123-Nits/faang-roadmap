"How does Kafka guarantee ordering?" — per partition only; same key →same partition; idempotent producer prevents retry reordering.

"Can a consumer group have more consumers than partitions?" — yes, extras sit idle.
"Exactly‐once?" — idempotent producer + transactions +

read_committed, within Kafka. With external side effects: at‐least‐

once + idempotency.

"Kafka vs RabbitMQ?" — see Part 4: log with replay vs smart broker with per‐message routing/acks.
"Why is the consumer lagging?" — slow processing, too few partitions/consumers, rebalancing loops, hot partition, GC pauses,

downstream throttling.
