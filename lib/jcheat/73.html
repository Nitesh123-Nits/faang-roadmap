10.16kubectl Debugging Workflow

StepCommand
1 What's wrong?kubectl get pods -n shop -o wide (STATUS, RESTARTS, NODE)
2 Why? (events!)kubectl describe pod <pod> -n shop →Events, Last State, Exit Code, Reason
3 Current logskubectl logs <pod> -c app -n shop --tail=200 -f
4 Crashed container's logskubectl logs <pod> -c app --previous
5 Namespace eventskubectl get events -n shop --sort-by=.lastTimestamp
6 Resource usagekubectl top pod -n shop --containers · kubectl top node
7 Exec / port‐forwardkubectl exec -it <pod> -- sh · kubectl port-forward svc/orders 8081:8081

9 Service wiringkubectl get endpointslices -l kubernetes.io/service-name=orders (empty = selector/readiness problem)
10 JVM inside podkubectl exec <pod> -- jcmd 1 Thread.print (if the JDK tools exist in the image)
