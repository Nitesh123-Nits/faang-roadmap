"Why not just use a database row as a leader lock?" — you can (with leases and fencing), and it's often fine. The hard part isn't electing a leader; it's ensuring the old leader stops acting — which requires fencing at the resource.

5.9Time, Clock Skew and Ordering

Wall clocks drift and jump (NTP corrections, leap seconds, VM migrations). Never order events across machines by wall‐
