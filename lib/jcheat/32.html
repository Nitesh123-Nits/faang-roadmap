Sagas give ACD, not I: no isolation. Other transactions may see intermediate states (order PENDING, stock reserved). Countermeasures: semantic locks (status fields), commutative updates, re‐reading values, and versioning.

4.11Transactional Outbox and Inbox

Transactional outbox: atomically change state AND publish an event

Order Service

@Transactional

Guarantee: at-least-once publication (relay may resend after crash) →consumers must be idempotent.

Avoids dual-write bug: DB commit succeeds but Kafka send fails (or vice versa).

Outbox. Write business change and event in one local transaction; a relay publishes later.
