Core Model: Brokers, Topics, Partitions, Offsets, Replicas

What it is:a distributed, partitioned, replicated **append‐only log**. Producers append records; consumers read at their own pace by offset; records stay for a retention period regardless of consumption.Why it matters:durable decoupling, replay, high throughput, ordered streams per key.

Producer →broker →consumer. Each partition has one leader and followers; acks=all plus min.insync.replicas defines durability. Ordering exists per partition only.

ConceptMeaning
Topicnamed stream, split into partitions (unit of parallelism and ordering)
Offsetposition of a record within one partition (monotonic, per partition)
Replica / ISRcopies of a partition on other brokers; in‐sync replicas are caught up with the leader
Leaderhandles produce (and by default fetch) for a partition; followers replicate
High‐watermarklast offset replicated to all ISR →visible to consumers
Controller / KRaftmetadata quorum (Raft). Kafka 4.0+ runs only in KRaft mode — ZooKeeper removed
