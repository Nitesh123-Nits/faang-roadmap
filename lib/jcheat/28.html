DECISION
RULE

Split a service out when it has a clear bounded context , an owning team, and a real driver (independent scaling, release cadence, compliance isolation, different tech). "Microservices for modernisation" without those drivers yields a distributed monolith: services that must deploy together and call each other synchronously in chains.
