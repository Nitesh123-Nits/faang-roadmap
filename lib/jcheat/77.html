11.5Consistency, Availability, Fault Tolerance and DR

ConcernTypical decision
Consistency vs availabilitystrong for money/inventory/auth; eventual for feeds, counts, search, analytics
Single‐AZ failure≥3 replicas across AZs; DB with synchronous standby in another AZ
Region failureactive‐passive with async replication (RPO > 0) or active‐active with conflict strategy (costly)
Dependency failuretimeouts, circuit breakers, graceful degradation, queues for retry
Data lossbackups + tested restores , point‐in‐time recovery, immutable/offsite copies
Bad deploycanary, feature flags, fast rollback, backward‐compatible schemas

Security & observability by default: authN at edge + service identity inside, encryption in transit/at rest, least privilege, audit logs; RED metrics per endpoint, traces across hops, SLO‐based alerts. Cost & operational complexity: every extra datastore is a new on‐call skill — justify it.
