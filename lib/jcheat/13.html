Happens‐before. Everything Thread A wrote before the volatile write is visible to Thread B after it reads that volatile and sees the new value.

Visibility: without a happens‐before edge, another thread may never see a write (CPU caches, JIT hoisting a read out of a

loop).

Ordering: the compiler and CPU reorder freely within a thread unless HB constraints forbid it.
