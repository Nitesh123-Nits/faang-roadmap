5.2CAP Theorem and Its Limitations

CAP: under a network Partition, a replicated register must choose between Consistency (linearizability) and Availability (every non‐failed node answers). It says nothing when there is no partition.

COMMON
MISTAKES
"Pick two of three" — partitions are not optional, so the real

choice is C or A during a partition.

Labelling whole databases "CP" or "AP": most systems are

configurable per operation (e.g. read/write consistency levels) and many offer neither strict C nor strict A.

Equating CAP's "C" with ACID's "C" (constraint validity) —

unrelated.

PACELC
THE MORE USEFUL
FRAMING

If Partitioned, choose A or C; Else, choose Latency or Consistency. Synchronous replication costs latency on every write even on a healthy network — that's the trade‐off you pay daily.
