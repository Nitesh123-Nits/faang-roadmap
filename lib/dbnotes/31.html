WORKED EXAMPLES

Non-repeatable read (Read Committed).

-- T1: SELECT salary --. -> 5000
-- T2: UPDATE salary = 6000; COMMIT;
-- T1: SELECT salary --. -> 6000   (same query, different result)

Phantom read (Repeatable Read).

-- T1: SELECT * FROM employees WHERE dept = 'IT';   -- 10 rows
-- T2: INSERT new IT employee; COMMIT;
-- T1: SELECT * FROM employees WHERE dept = 'IT';   -- 11 rows (phantom)

CHOOSING A LEVEL

Strong consistency, such as banking, means a higher level (Serializable). Higher performance means a lower level (Read Committed). Higher isolation costs more locking or versioning overhead.

R E M E M B E R . Choose the isolation level by requirement, and be ready to say

which anomaly each level allows.
