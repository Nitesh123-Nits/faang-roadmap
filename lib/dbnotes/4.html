CHAPTER 02

Data Modeling

The blueprint of your data. Good models make systems easier to build, scale and maintain.

Data modeling is the process of defining structure, relationships and rules for data in a system. Its goals: organise data logically, reduce redundancy, ensure integrity, support queries and performance, and adapt to change.

THREE LEVELS OF MODEL

Other model families: dimensional, object, graph, document, key‐value.

Understand the business. Gather requirements and data sources.
Identify entities. Find the key data objects (customer, order, product).
Define attributes. The properties of each entity.
Define relationships. Associations and cardinality.
Define constraints and rules. Keys, business rules, validation.
Validate and refine. Review, normalise, optimise.
KEY CONCEPTS
Entity: a distinguishable object or concept.
Attribute: a property or characteristic of an entity.
Relationship: an association between entities.
