GOLDEN RULES
There is no "best" database, only the best fit for a specific workload.
Understand your data, access patterns, scaling needs and consistency require‐

ments.

Use the simplest database that solves your problem today.
Many systems require multiple databases (polyglot persistence).
Re‐evaluate as your system grows.
