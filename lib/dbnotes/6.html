DIMENSIONAL MODELING (ANALYTICS)

A star schema puts a central fact table (measurable events, numeric) in the middle and surrounds it with dimension tables that provide context: date, product, store, customer. It is simple and fast for reporting queries.

MODELING CHECKLIST
All required entities and attributes captured
Primary keys defined for every entity
Relationships and cardinalities correct
Constraints (PK, FK, unique, not null) defined
Normalised appropriately
Indexes planned for performance
Data growth and change considered
Model aligned with business rules and validated with stakeholders
COMMON PITFALLS

Over‐normalisation hurts performance. Under‐normalisation causes redundancy. Missing constraints let bad data in. Wrong data types waste space and break com‐ parisons. Ignoring future scale and having no indexing strategy both surface late and cost most. Not involving stakeholders produces a clean model of the wrong thing.
