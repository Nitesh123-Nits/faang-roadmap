Understand your workload. What are you building? What type of data? Read/write pattern? Scale (size and growth)? Consistency requirements? Budget and team expertise?
Identify data requirements. Data model, relationships, transactions, query patterns, scalability, availability, consistency (ACID or BASE), durability, latency.
Match a database type. Relational, key-value, document, wide-column, graph, time series, search/analytics, in-memory.
Evaluate and decide. Compare options, prototype or proof-of-concept, benchmark, check the ecosystem, consider operational cost and team skill, then make the call.
