CHAPTER 24

Change Data Capture (CDC)

CDC is a design pattern that identifies and captures changes made to data in a source system and makes them available to downstream systems in real time or near real time.

HOW CDC WORKS
Detect change. Inserts, updates and deletes are detected in the source, via logs, triggers or timestamps.
Capture. Changes are captured in the order they occur.
Deliver. Changes are streamed or published to target systems.
Consume. Downstream systems consume and apply the changes.
