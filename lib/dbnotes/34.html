How @Transactional Works Internally

AOP proxies, transaction interceptors and a PlatformTransactionManager .

The client calls the method on a Spring bean. The call goes through the proxy (JDK dynamic proxy or CGLIB). The proxy delegates to TransactionInterceptor (a MethodInterceptor ). The interceptor reads the transaction attributes (propagation, isolation, timeout, read‐ only) and calls PlatformTransactionManager.getTransaction() . A new transaction is created, or an existing one is joined, according to propagation. Re‐ sources (connection, session) are bound to the current thread via TransactionSynchronizationManager . The target method, your business logic, runs. On success the transaction commits. On an exception it rolls back according to the roll‐ back rules. Resources are unbound and cleaned up from the thread, and the result (or exception) re‐ turns to the caller.
