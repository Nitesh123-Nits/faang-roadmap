MONITORING AND OBSERVABILITY

Watch slow queries, lock waits, connections, CPU/memory, disk/IOPS and replication lag. Tools: Prometheus, Grafana, pg_stat_statements , performance_schema , CloudWatch.

COMMON ANTI-PATTERNS
SELECT * from large tables
Missing indexes on joins and filters
Too many indexes
No LIMIT on large result sets
Overuse of transactions
Large unbounded text/blob columns in hot tables
Ignoring query plans
Not analysing slow queries
PERFORMANCE CHECKLIST
Schema designed for queries Indexes created and reviewed Queries optimised and tested Caching implemented where needed DB configuration tuned and documented Monitoring and alerts in place Capacity planning and load testing done

GOLDEN RULE

Measure, analyse, optimise, validate, monitor, repeat. Optimise the right queries: the 80/20 rule applies. Good design + good queries + good indexes + good infrastructure = high performance.

R E M E M B E R . Do not tune what you have not measured.
