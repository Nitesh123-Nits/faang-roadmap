QUICK MEMORY GUIDE
Block: like pieces of a hard disk.
File: like files in computer folders.
Object: like objects in a bucket with an ID.
Lake: store everything, raw. Warehouse: store clean, structured data. Lakehouse:

modern hybrid of both.

R E M E M B E R . Choose storage on performance, cost and use case. Object storage is

the foundation of data lakes, and modern architectures are moving towards the lakehouse.
