SELECTION FRAMEWORK

Pick in three steps.

Access pattern. Key lookup: Redis. Range queries: PostgreSQL. Massive writes: Cassandra. Search: Elasticsearch. Analytics: ClickHouse. Relationships: Neo4j. Scaling need. Vertical scaling: PostgreSQL. Horizontal scaling: Cassandra. Global distribution: Cosmos DB, DynamoDB. In-memory speed: Redis. Distributed SQL: CockroachDB. Consistency need. Strong (ACID, critical data): PostgreSQL, MySQL. Eventual (high availability, high throughput): Cassandra, DynamoDB, MongoDB.
