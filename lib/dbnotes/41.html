PROS AND CONS OF GETTING IT RIGHT

A well-chosen database gives you performance, scale and reliability, better developer productivity, lower operational cost and a future-proof architecture. A wrong choice costs you performance, latency and money, a hard migration later, and developer frustration.

GOLDEN RULE

There is no "one size fits all" database. Choose the database that best fits your data, workload and team. You can always combine multiple databases for different needs.

R E M E M B E R . Start simple, measure, iterate, and re‐evaluate as your application

and scale evolve. Be use‐case driven, not product driven.
