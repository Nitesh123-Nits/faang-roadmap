How to use this map

Knowing a technology is not the same as knowing when to use it. For every name below, be able to say four things:

The problem it solves
When to choose it
When to avoid it
What it costs
G O A L

Aim for 418 concepts, not 418 deep dives. Know the foundations cold, and the rest well enough to compare and choose.

1 · Problem What are we actually solving?

↓

2 · Constraints Team, budget, deadline, compliance

↓

3 · Alternatives At least two real options

↓

4 · Trade-offs What do we give up?

↓

6 · Operations Who runs it at 3 a.m.?

↓

7 · Cost Build, run and change

↓
