Register initialisers: ApplicationContextInitializer s run.
Load bean definitions: scan packages, process @Configuration , @Bean , @Import .
Invoke BeanFactoryPostProcessor s: modify bean definitions.
Register BeanPostProcessor s.
Instantiate singleton beans (lazy beans excluded).
