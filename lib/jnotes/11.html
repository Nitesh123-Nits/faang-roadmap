JDK ⊃ JRE ⊃ JVM. Develop → Run → Execute.

JVM is the heart: memory management, class loading, security, garbage collection.
JRE is the body: JVM plus the libraries ( rt.jar or modules) needed to run programs.
JDK is the complete toolkit: javac , jar , javadoc , jdb , jlink , jdeps , jps , jstat ,

jmap , jcmd .
