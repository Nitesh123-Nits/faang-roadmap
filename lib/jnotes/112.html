CHAPTER 63

Transaction Propagation

Propagation controls how a method annotated with @Transactional behaves when it is called from another @Transactional method. It defines how a new transactional method joins, creates or suspends an existing transaction.

Spring manages transactions through the PlatformTransactionManager .
Default propagation = REQUIRED .
