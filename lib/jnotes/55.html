ExecutorService vs CompletableFuture

They are not competitors; they solve different problems and work best together.

ONE-LINER.

ExecutorService controls execution; CompletableFuture controls

asynchronous composition. ExecutorService tells WHO will do the work. CompletableFuture defines HOW and WHEN the workflow and results are composed.

USE EXECUTORSERVICE WHEN

You need explicit control over a thread pool, you are submitting independent

Runnable / Callable tasks, you need bounded concurrency, or you want worker/

task‐processing patterns.
