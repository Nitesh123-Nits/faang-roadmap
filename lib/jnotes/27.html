CHAPTER 18

The Collections Framework

Everything in the Collections Framework implements Iterable . Map is not a subinterface of Collection , but is part of the framework.

DETAILS THAT MATTER

HashSet is backed by a HashMap internally.
LinkedHashSet is backed by a LinkedHashMap .
TreeSet is backed by a TreeMap (red‐black tree).
PriorityQueue is not thread‐safe; use PriorityBlockingQueue .
Vector , Stack and Hashtable are legacy; prefer ArrayList , ArrayDeque and

ConcurrentHashMap .

Concurrent collections live in java.util.concurrent .
Since Java 8, most interfaces have default methods.

MARKER INTERFACES

Serializable (serialisation), Cloneable (cloning), RandomAccess (fast index access), Comparable (natural ordering), Comparator (custom ordering).
