IMPORTANT NOTES

Always call countDown() in a finally block to avoid deadlock.
If a thread waiting on await() is interrupted, InterruptedException is thrown.
In CyclicBarrier , if one thread is interrupted or times out, the others get a

BrokenBarrierException .

For a dynamic number of parties or more complex phases, consider Phaser .
