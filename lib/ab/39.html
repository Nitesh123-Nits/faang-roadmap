INTERVIEW TIP — CROSS-CUTTING PATTERN

Start from access patterns and invariants, not products. Say which operations need strong guarantees and which tolerate staleness; that split drives nearly every data decision.

COMMON MISTAKE

Choosing a database for scale you do not have, then paying for it in lost joins and transactions. Scale vertically, cache, and replicate before you shard or switch models.
