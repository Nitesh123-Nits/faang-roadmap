Orchestration vs Choreography

Decide whether a coordinator directs a multi-service workflow or services react to each other’s events.

THE 30-SECOND ANSWER

Orchestration uses a central coordinator that tells each service what to do and tracks workflow state, which makes complex flows visible and easy to compensate but concentrates logic and risk. Choreography has services publish events and react independently, which keeps coupling low but makes the overall flow implicit and harder to debug. Use orchestration for complex, auditable workflows and choreography for simple, loosely coupled reactions.
