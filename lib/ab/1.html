How to use this book

Every comparison follows the same five-part template, kept short on purpose so you can revise fast. Read the 30-second answer first; use the quick revision card on the final pass before an interview.

#SECTIONWHAT IT GIVES YOU

4Decision frameworkExplicit rules for choosing, including when a hybrid wins
5Quick revision cardSix lines to scan in the last five minutes before an interview

Visual language used in every diagram

Dark ink always means Option A and teal always means Option B. Amber marks failure or risk. Gray marks asynchronous flow. Solid arrows are synchronous calls; dashed cyan arrows are asynchronous events.

ACCURACY CONVENTIONS

Guarantee means documented behavior you can rely on. Typical means common behavior that depends on configuration or workload. Implementation-dependent means it varies by product or version. Latency and throughput statements assume the conditions given in the text, and no chapter claims one option is universally better.
