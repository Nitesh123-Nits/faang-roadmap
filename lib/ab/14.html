Layered Architecture vs Hexagonal Architecture

Decide whether code is organized by technical layers or around a domain core isolated from frameworks by ports and adapters.

THE 30-SECOND ANSWER

Layered architecture stacks presentation, business, and data-access layers with dependencies pointing downward, which is simple and familiar but tends to make business logic depend on persistence. Hexagonal (ports and adapters) puts the domain at the center, defines ports as interfaces the domain owns, and implements them with adapters for web, messaging, and databases, so dependencies point inward. Use layered for simple CRUD, hexagonal for rich domains.
