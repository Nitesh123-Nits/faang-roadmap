SQL vs NoSQL

Choose between a relational model with joins and rich transactions and a family of stores built around specific access patterns and horizontal scale.

THE 30-SECOND ANSWER

SQL databases model data as related tables with a schema, joins, and multi-row ACID transactions, and they answer ad-hoc queries well. NoSQL is a family (key-value, document, wide-column, graph) that trades some of that generality for flexible schemas or predictable latency at scale, usually by designing the data around known queries. Default to a relational database; pick NoSQL for a named access pattern or scale need it serves better.
