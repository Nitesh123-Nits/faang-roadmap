REST vs gRPC

Pick the protocol for synchronous calls: ubiquitous HTTP/JSON resources or schema-first RPC over HTTP/2.

THE 30-SECOND ANSWER

REST uses HTTP semantics and typically JSON, giving universal compatibility, caching, and easy debugging. gRPC uses HTTP/2 with Protocol Buffers, giving a schema-first contract, generated clients, streaming, and compact binary messages. gRPC suits internal service-to-service calls where you control both ends; REST suits public APIs, browsers, and third parties. CHOOSE A WHENPublic or browser-facing APIs; caching and simplicity matter.
CHOOSE B WHENInternal, latency-sensitive, polyglot calls; streaming or strict contracts needed.
