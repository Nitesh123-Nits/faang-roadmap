Synchronous vs Asynchronous Communication

Decide whether a caller waits for the answer or hands off work and continues.

THE 30-SECOND ANSWER

Synchronous communication blocks the caller until the callee responds, which gives an immediate answer and simple error handling but couples the two in time. Asynchronous communication hands off a message or event through a broker and continues, which absorbs spikes and decouples availability at the cost of eventual completion, duplicates, and harder debugging. Use sync when the answer is needed now; use async for decoupling and long-running work.

Synchronous vs Asynchronous Communication

QUICK REVISION CARD
CORE DIFFERENCE

Caller waits for the answer versus hands off a message and continues.

CHOOSE A WHEN

Result needed now; short bounded operation; reliable dependency; simplicity wins.

CHOOSE B WHEN

Bursty or long-running work; independent availability; multiple consumers; pending state acceptable.

BIGGEST TRADE-OFF

Immediate answer and simplicity versus decoupling and resilience at the cost of eventual completion.

KEY FAILURE MODE

Retry storms and cascades (sync); duplicates, poison messages, and lag (async).

ONE-LINE ANSWER

“Sync when the user is waiting on the answer; async when the system is reacting to a fact. Make async consumers idempotent.”
