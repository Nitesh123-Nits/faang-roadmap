Soft Delete vs Hard Delete

Keep deleted rows flagged for recovery and audit, or remove them for real.

THE 30-SECOND ANSWER

Soft delete marks a row as deleted (a timestamp or flag) and hides it from normal queries, allowing recovery and audit trails at the cost of larger tables, filter discipline, and retained data. Hard delete removes the row, which is simple and supports privacy erasure but is irreversible except through backups. Choose per entity: soft delete where recovery or history matters, hard delete (or later purge) where retention is a liability.
