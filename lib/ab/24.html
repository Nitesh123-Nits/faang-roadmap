Key-Value Store vs Wide-Column Store

Decide between simple one-key lookups and partitioned, sorted rows built for very large write- heavy datasets.

THE 30-SECOND ANSWER

A key-value store gives one value per key. A wide-column store (Cassandra, Bigtable-style, HBase) groups rows under a partition key and keeps them sorted by clustering columns, so you can range-scan within a partition and write at very high rates across many nodes. Choose wide-column for time-series, event, or feed-like data queried by partition and time range; choose key-value for simple lookups.

ONE-LINE ANSWER

“Wide-column when you write a lot and read by partition and time; key-value when you only look up by key.”
