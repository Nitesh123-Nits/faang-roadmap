Optimistic Locking vs Pessimistic Locking

Handle concurrent updates by detecting conflicts at write time or by preventing them with locks.

THE 30-SECOND ANSWER

Optimistic locking assumes conflicts are rare: read a version, and on update succeed only if the version is unchanged, otherwise retry. Pessimistic locking assumes conflicts are likely: lock the row first so others wait. Optimistic scales better under low contention; pessimistic is safer and simpler when many writers hit the same rows and retries would thrash.
