gRPC vs Message-Based Communication

Choose between a fast typed RPC to a specific service and publishing messages that a broker stores and delivers.

THE 30-SECOND ANSWER

gRPC is a direct typed call to a known service that needs to be reachable and responsive now, and gives a quick answer or error. Message-based communication writes to a durable broker that buffers, fans out, retries, and can replay, so producers and consumers need not be available together. Use gRPC for lowlatency request/response; use messaging for decoupled, bursty, or multi-consumer work.
