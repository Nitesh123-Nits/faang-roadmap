SOA vs Microservices

Compare enterprise-wide service integration through a central bus with small, independently owned services.

THE 30-SECOND ANSWER

SOA organizes reusable, coarse-grained enterprise services, usually integrated through a central bus that handles routing, transformation, and policy. Microservices use small services around bounded contexts, with logic in the services and simple communication between them. The boundary is blurry because microservices grew out of service orientation. Choose by integration context: heterogeneous legacy estates lean SOA, product teams lean microservices.
