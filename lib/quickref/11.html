CHAPTER 04

Extract Methods, Classes and Responsibilities

Extraction is how you give a block of logic a name. Do it when the name earns its place.

Decision guide

Ask these in order.

Does the block have a meaningful, independent

purpose? Yes: extract a method, and give the block a name.

Is it a distinct responsibility or business

concept? Yes: extract a class, and let it own the concept.

Otherwise keep the implementation simple.

Design principles behind the guide: one reason to change per class; keep HTTP, rules and persistence apart; members of a class belong together; depend on small interfaces and inject them; inherit only for a true "is-a".
