Extract a method when…

It performs several logically distinct steps.
A block has a meaningful, independent purpose.
The same logic is duplicated.
You must scroll or re-read to follow it.
Nested conditions hide the main path.
A section deserves a descriptive name.

Create a separate class when…

It mixes unrelated responsibilities.
Parts change for different business reasons.
A dependency serves only one separate concern.
The logic needs independent testing or reuse.
A business concept deserves its own abstraction.
The class is hard to understand or maintain.
