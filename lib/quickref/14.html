Extract class: split by reason to change

Before: one OrderService with four reasons to change.

OrderService
  validate(order)        // business rules
  calculatePrice(order)  // pricing policy
  save(order)            // persistence
  sendConfirmation()     // email channel

After: OrderService orchestrates the use case and delegates to cohesive collaborators: business rules, pricing policy, persistence, email channel.

When not to extract

Not every 3-line block needs a method. Extract when it earns a name, not to hit a length target.
Not every field deserves its own class. Avoid abstractions with a single, speculative use.
Don't create classes just to follow a pattern, or one-method wrappers with no real responsibility.

Remember. Extract to reveal intent, not to hit a number.
