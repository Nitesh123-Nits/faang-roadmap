The release column shows semantic-release defaults: breaking change gives MAJOR, feat gives MINOR, fix and perf give PATCH. *Custom types

need a releaseRules entry and a commitlint type- enum entry (see Automation below).

Commit message structure

Header (required), body, footers:

feat(order-service)!: replace REST callback
    with Kafka event

Order confirmation now publishes
OrderConfirmedEvent to the orders.confirmed
topic instead of calling the billing webhook.

BREAKING CHANGE: billing-service must consume
orders.confirmed.
Refs: PAY-142
Imperative, lowercase description, no trailing period.
Short header: aim for 72 characters (commitlint limit: 100).
Scope is the service, module or layer, in kebabcase.
Body explains why, after a blank line.
! or a BREAKING CHANGE: footer means MAJOR.
One logical change per commit; ticket in a footer.
