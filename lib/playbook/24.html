Handy conversions

1 day ≈ 86,400 s, so 1 M requests/day ≈ 12 req/s average.
Peak is typically 2–5× the average; design for peak.
1KB×1M=1GB.1MB×1M=1TB.
A single well-tuned database node handles low thousands of simple writes/s; an inmemory cache handles 100k+ ops/s per node.
Availability: 99.9% ≈ 8.8 h downtime/year; 99.99% ≈ 53 min; 99.999% ≈ 5 min.

Worked example A service with 50 M daily users, 20 requests each: 1 B requests/day ≈ 11,600 req/s average, ~40k req/s at 3.5× peak. At 2 KB per response, that is about 80 MB/s of egress at peak. These figures are estimates; state your assumptions aloud and refine them.

U S E

Numbers here are rough, widely quoted approximations. Their value is the order of magnitude, not the digits.
