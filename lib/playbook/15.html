Load balancing

L4 (TCP) is fast and protocol-agnostic. L7 (HTTP) can route by path, header or cookie.
Algorithms: round robin, least connections, weighted, consistent hash (for cache affinity). Least-connections copes better with uneven request cost.
Health checks and connection draining make deploys and failures invisible to users.
The balancer itself must not be a single point of failure: run it redundantly, across zones.
