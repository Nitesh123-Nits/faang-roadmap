How JWT Works

THE IDEA

A signed, self-contained token that lets servers stay stateless.

HOW IT WORKS

5

REMEMBER
A JWT is signed, not encrypted. Keep secrets out.
Short expiry + refresh rotation; pin the algorithm.
Stateless scale costs instant revocation.
DEEP DIVE

A JSON Web Token carries the user's identity and claims inside the token itself. The server signs it; any service that can verify the signature can trust the claims without a session lookup. That is why JWTs suit horizontally scaled and microservice systems.

Structure Three Base64URL parts joined by dots: header (algorithm, key id), payload (claims such as sub , exp , iss , aud , roles) and signature. The payload is encoded, not encrypted — never put secrets in it.
