CHAPTER VII

Generative Machinery

The families of model responsible for producing wholly new content — text, image, sound.

No. 45

Generative AI

Models trained to produce new, original content — text, images, audio, or code — rather than merely classifying or scoring existing input.

No. 46

GAN (Generative Adversarial Network)

Two networks trained against each other — a generator producing fakes, a discriminator trying to catch them — until the generator's output is convincing.

No. 47

Diffusion Model

A generative approach that learns to reverse a gradual noising process, starting from pure noise and iteratively refining it into a coherent image or signal.

No. 48

Latent Space

A compressed, abstract coordinate space in which a model represents its data; moving through this space produces smooth, meaningful variations in generated output.
