CHAPTER V

Large Language Models

The vocabulary an engineer meets daily when integrating chat and completion models into real software.

No. 29

LLM (Large Language Model)

A Transformer-based model trained on vast text corpora to predict the next token, giving rise to broad abilities in writing, reasoning, and code.

No. 30

Token & Tokenization

Text is broken into tokens — pieces of words or characters — before a model can process it. Cost and context limits are almost always measured in tokens, not characters.

No. 31

Context Window

The maximum number of tokens (prompt plus response) a model can consider at once. Exceeding it means earlier content is truncated or dropped.

ENGINEER’S NOTE

treat it like a fixed-size buffer you must budget carefully — much like managing a limited request payload.

No. 32

Embedding

A dense vector of numbers representing the meaning of a word, sentence, or document, positioned such that semantically similar items sit close together in that vector space.

No. 33

Prompt & Prompt Engineering

The prompt is the input text given to a model; prompt engineering is the practice of crafting that input deliberately to reliably steer the model's output.

No. 34

Zero-Shot / Few-Shot

Zero-shot: asking a model to perform a task with no examples given. Few-shot: supplying a handful of examples within the prompt itself to demonstrate the desired pattern.

No. 35

Chain-of-Thought (CoT)

Prompting a model to reason through intermediate steps aloud before giving a final answer, which tends to improve results on multi-step problems.

No. 36

Temperature, Top-p & Top-k

Sampling controls governing randomness at inference time: temperature scales overall unpredictability, while top-p and top-k restrict which candidate tokens are eligible to be chosen next.

No. 37

Hallucination

A confident but factually incorrect or fabricated output — a known failure mode arising because the model optimizes for plausible text, not verified truth.

No. 38

System Prompt

A privileged instruction set placed ahead of user input to establish a model's persona, rules, and constraints for the remainder of a conversation.
