Schools of Learning

Every model is trained under one of these disciplines, or some blend thereof. Knowing which is in use tells you what data was needed and what the model can be trusted to do.

No. 9

Supervised Learning

Training on labeled examples — inputs paired with known correct outputs — so the model learns to reproduce that mapping on new data.

No. 10

Unsupervised Learning

Training on unlabeled data to discover structure on its own — clusters, groupings, or compressed representations — with no "correct answer" supplied.

No. 11

Self-Supervised Learning

A hybrid where labels are generated automatically from the data itself — for instance, predicting the next word in a sentence. This is how most large language models are pre-trained.

No. 12

Reinforcement Learning (RL)

An agent learns by taking actions in an environment and receiving rewards or penalties, gradually favoring the actions that yield greater long-term reward.

No. 13

Transfer Learning

Taking a model already trained on one large task and adapting it to a new, related task — reusing learned knowledge instead of starting from a blank slate.

No. 14

Fine-Tuning

Continuing to train an existing pre-trained model on a smaller, specific dataset so it specializes to a narrower task, domain, or tone.
