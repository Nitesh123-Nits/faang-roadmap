CHAPTER X

Safety, Ethics & Governance

The considerations any engineer shipping AI-driven features is now expected to reason about, not merely the model's authors.

No. 60

Bias (Algorithmic)

Systematic skew in a model's outputs, typically inherited from imbalances or prejudices present in its training data.

No. 61

Alignment

The broad effort to ensure a model's behavior matches human intentions and values, rather than optimizing its stated objective in unintended ways.

No. 62

Guardrails

Engineering controls — filters, validators, refusal policies — layered around a model in production to keep its behavior within acceptable bounds.

No. 63

Prompt Injection

An attack in which malicious instructions are smuggled into content a model processes (e.g. a webpage or document), attempting to hijack its behavior — the AIera analogue of injection attacks in traditional security.

No. 64

Explainability (XAI)

Techniques and tools aimed at making a model's internal reasoning or decision process interpretable to humans, rather than treating it as an opaque black box.
