CHAPTER IX

Measures & Evaluation

How the quality of a model, or of any single prediction, is scored and compared.

No. 56

Accuracy, Precision & Recall

Accuracy is overall correctness; precision is how many predicted positives were truly positive; recall is how many actual positives the model successfully found.

No. 57

F1 Score

The harmonic mean of precision and recall, used as a single balanced score when both false positives and false negatives matter.

No. 58

Perplexity

A measure of how "surprised" a language model is by real text; lower perplexity means the model assigns higher probability to what actually occurs.

No. 59

Benchmark

A standardized test set (e.g. MMLU, HumanEval, GSM8K) used to compare different models' capabilities on a common, reproducible footing.
