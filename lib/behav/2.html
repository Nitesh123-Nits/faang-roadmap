Top Behavioral Questions

Have a real story ready for each of these ten.

Tell me about yourself.Walk me through your background, key experiences, and what brings you here today.

The STAR Method

Structure your answers for clarity and impact.

SituationSet the context. Where and when did it happen?
TaskWhat was your responsibility or goal?
ActionWhat steps did you take? Be specific.
ResultWhat was the outcome? Share impact, metrics, or key learnings.
TIPS TO ACE BEHAVIORAL INTERVIEWS
Understand the question. Clarify if needed.
Use the STAR method for structured answers.
Be specific and honest. Share real examples.
Focus on your actions, not the team's.
Quantify results when possible.
Show your thought process and learnings.
Keep it concise: 1.5–2 minutes is ideal.
Practice out loud. Preparation builds confidence.

Extra tips & reminders

Review your past projects and experiences.
Prepare stories in advance for each theme.
Tailor examples to the role and company.
Stay positive. No blaming or badmouthing.
End with key learnings or impact.

Remember: “Anyone can say they are a good problem solver or a great leader. Your stories prove it.”
