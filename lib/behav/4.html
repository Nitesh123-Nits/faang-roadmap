10 Powerful Questions

Smart questions show ownership, curiosity, business understanding and long-term thinking. For all roles.

If I join this role, what would success look like in the first 90 days?Shows you are thinking about delivering value and taking ownership.

Demonstrates problem-solving mindset and helps you understand real work.

Makes the interviewer imagine you in the role and creates a consultative discussion.

Shows engineering maturity and starts a meaningful technical discussion.

Great for senior backend / technical roles. Reveals system design complexity.

Shows understanding of real-world engineering tradeoffs.

Shows strategic thinking and long-term interest.

Signals leadership potential and interest in decision making.

Senior & Staff Candidates

Additional questions for senior and staff-level roles.

What are the highest-impact initiatives planned for the team this year?Shows you think about impact and priorities.

Shows interest in growth and leadership path.

Shows interest in cross-team collaboration and the bigger picture.

THE POWERFUL CLOSING QUESTION

“Based on our discussion today, do you have any concerns about my experience or fit for the role that I can address right now?”

Shows confidence.
Gives you a chance to handle objections.
Many candidates never ask this.

Best 5 for Software Engineers

If you only have time for five, ask these.

1

What would success look like in the first 90 days?

2

What are the biggest challenges the team is solving right now?

3

What differentiates top performers on this team?

4

How does the team balance feature delivery with technical debt?

5

Based on our discussion, do you have any concerns about my fit that I can address?

QUESTIONS TO AVOID
How soon do people get promoted?
How many leaves do I get? (Unless discussing offer details)
Do I have to work hard?
What time can I leave?
Questions already answered during the interview.

Asking thoughtful questions shows that you are not just looking for a job, you are looking to make an impact and grow with the organization.
