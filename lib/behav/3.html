PART 2 · LEADERSHIP PRINCIPLES

Amazon Leadership Principles

The foundation of how Amazon thinks, decides, and acts. Principles 1–8.

Customer ObsessionLeaders start with the customer and work backwards. They earn trust, listen deeply, and deliver what customers need, often before they ask.
Understand customers deeply. Earn trust through actions. Innovate to delight customers.
OwnershipLeaders are owners. They think long term and don't say “that's not my job.” They act on behalf of the entire company.
Take ownership beyond your role. Solve problems without waiting. Think long term.
Invent and SimplifyLeaders expect and create innovation. They look for simpler solutions and remove complexity.
Challenge the status quo. Encourage new ideas. Simplify and focus on what matters.
Are Right, A LotLeaders have strong judgment and make highquality decisions with available information.
Make decisions with conviction. Disagree and commit. Learn and adjust quickly.
Learn and Be CuriousLeaders never stop learning. They are curious about new possibilities and seek to improve themselves.
Ask questions. Seek different perspectives. Learn continuously.
Hire and Develop the BestLeaders raise the bar with every hire and invest in developing people to reach their full potential.
Hire the best. Give honest feedback. Develop future leaders.
Insist on the Highest StandardsLeaders set high standards and continuously raise the bar. They focus on quality, always.
Deliver high-quality results. Pay attention to detail. Continuously improve.
Think BigLeaders think beyond today and beyond their team. They look for big, bold opportunities.
Think long term. Take smart risks. Imagine what's possible.
PART 2 · LEADERSHIP PRINCIPLES

Amazon Leadership Principles

Principles 9–16.

Bias for ActionSpeed matters. Leaders move fast, make decisions, and adjust along the way.
Act quickly. Don't wait for perfection. Learn from doing.
FrugalityLeaders do more with less. They are good stewards of resources and respect the company's money and tools.
Avoid waste. Be resourceful. Focus on impact, not expense.
Earn TrustLeaders are honest, transparent, and respectful. They listen, admit mistakes, and build strong relationships.
Communicate openly. Show respect. Build trust with actions.
Dive DeepLeaders operate at all levels and stay connected to details, metrics, and ground reality.
Dig into data. Understand root causes. Don't rely on assumptions.
Have Backbone; Disagree and CommitLeaders respectfully challenge decisions when needed, then fully commit to the decision, even if they disagreed.
Challenge decisions respectfully. Commit once decided. Put the team and mission first.
Deliver ResultsLeaders focus on outcomes and impact. They hold themselves and others accountable.
Set clear goals. Hold to commitments. Deliver for customers.
Strive to be Earth's Best EmployerLeaders create a safe, inclusive, and highperforming workplace where people can do their best work.
Value and respect people. Promote inclusion and diversity. Create a great place to work.
Success and Scale Bring Broad ResponsibilityAs we grow, our impact grows. Leaders act on behalf of society and future generations.
Think about the long-term impact. Be a good steward. Make sustainable choices.

Use these principles every day. They guide our decisions, actions, and culture.
