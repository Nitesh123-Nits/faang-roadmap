How to use this book

Most engineers’ favourite papers share a problem: they are dense, written for researchers, and 12 to 20 pages long. This book keeps what a working backend engineer needs: the problem, the core idea, how it works, the numbers that matter, and what you can reuse.

These chapters are original summaries and explanations, written to help you understand each paper before (or instead of) reading all of it. Figures were drawn for this book. Numbers are taken from the papers, and each chapter says when a point comes from outside the paper. For exact details and proofs, read the originals; links are at the back.

Reading order

Read straight through if you can. Part I gives you the vocabulary (ordering, consensus, quorums) that the rest assumes. Or jump to any chapter: each stands alone, and cross-references tell you where an idea came from. Short on time? The reading paths near the end suggest routes for interviews, databases, Google’s stack and event-driven work.
