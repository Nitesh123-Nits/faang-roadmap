60-Day Advanced DSA Roadmap

A deeper two-month programme: every chapter, five phases, and a timed exam at the end of each.

This plan is for engineers who want depth, not just coverage: every chapter gets its own day (or half day), every phase ends with a timed exam, and the error log drives what you repeat. Plan on 2.5 to 3 hours a day.

BlockMinutesWhat you do
Learn and implement45Read the chapters; re-type the main Java listing from memory the next morning
Chapter practice tables75 to 90Easy and Medium first; Hard only in the second half of the phase
Spaced review20Redo three problems from 3 and 7 days ago
Error log10Record the signal you missed, not the solution

WHEN YOU FALL BEHIND

If you fall behind, drop breadth, not review. Skip Hard problems and Part XII chapters before you skip the spaced reviews or the mock interviews: retention and speed under pressure are what the plan is buying.
