The Whole Book at a Glance

The handbook has twelve pattern parts with 107 pattern chapters, followed by Part XIII: three masterclass and toolkit chapters (M1 to M3) and six revision appendices (A to F). This is the complete edition. The table is the master table of contents. (Part I grew from 8 to 11 chapters after a gap analysis against popular pattern lists added Array Techniques, Strings and Matrix chapters.)
