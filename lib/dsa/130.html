30-Day DSA Practice Roadmap

A focused month: learn a pattern, solve guided and independent problems, revise, mix and mock.

The plan uses the book's own chapters and the Top 100 list. Each day states what to read, which problems to solve, and what to revise. Choose 30 days if you have an interview within a month and already know the basics; choose the 60-day plan (Appendix E) if you want durable problem-solving skill or are returning to DSA after a long break.

MOCK INTERVIEWS

Mock days (7, 14, 21, 29, 30) are the plan's quality control. Use a timer, say your reasoning out loud, and write the complexity before you code. If a mock goes badly, do not push forward: spend the next day redoing the misses.
