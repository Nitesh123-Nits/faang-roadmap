Part I Pattern Selector

When you meet an unfamiliar array or string problem, run it through this flowchart before you start coding. It resolves the eleven Part I patterns (Chapters 1–11); Part II adds a second flowchart for searching problems, Part III adds a third flowchart for lookup, counting and grouping, Part IV adds a fourth for linked lists, Part V adds a fifth for stacks, queues and design, Part VI adds a sixth for recursion and backtracking, Part VII adds a seventh for trees, Part VIII adds an eighth for graphs, Part IX adds a ninth for heaps and greedy algorithms, Part X adds a tenth for intervals, Part XI adds an eleventh for dynamic programming, Part XII adds a twelfth for advanced algorithms, and the final volume merges all twelve into one master selector.

TIP

Two patterns often combine: prefix sums + deque (shortest subarray with sum ≥ K), sliding window + HashMap (minimum window), monotonic stack + prefix/suffix reasoning (sum of subarray minimums). The flowchart picks the primary idea; the secondary pattern is in the practice tables.
