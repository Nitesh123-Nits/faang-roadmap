Video Conferencing

Zoom / Meet style

Functional Requirements
Schedule and join meetings via link
Real-time audio and video for up to 500 participants
Screen sharing
In-meeting chat and reactions
Recording and cloud storage
Waiting room, mute, host controls
Adaptive quality per participant
Non-Functional Requirements
Latency: end-to-end media < 200 ms
Quality: adaptive bitrate (simulcast / SVC)
Availability: 99.99% for in-progress meetings
Scalability: 300K concurrent meetings, regional SFUs
Resilience: survive packet loss up to 5%
Security: DTLS-SRTP, optional E2EE, auth to join
Efficiency: SFU forwarding, not decoding (cheap CPU)

SFU failure: clients re-join via Router to another SFU in seconds (ICE restart); roster in Redis
Network loss: adaptive bitrate, FEC and NACK; audio prioritised over video
Large rooms: cascaded SFUs across regions; only active speakers at high quality
Firewalls: TURN over TCP/443 as last-resort relay
