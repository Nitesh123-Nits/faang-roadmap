Distributed Lock Manager

Leases with fencing tokens

Functional Requirements
Acquire / release named locks (exclusive, shared)
Leases that expire automatically (TTL)
Keep-alive renewal while holder is alive
Fencing token with every grant
Wait queue and watch notifications
Re-entrant locks (optional) and try-lock
Observe lock owners for debugging
Non-Functional Requirements
Safety: at most one holder at a time (mutual exclusion)
Liveness: crashed holder's lock expires and is reclaimed
Consistency: linearizable via Raft
Availability: tolerates minority node failures
Latency: < 10 ms acquire in one region
Fencing: stale holders rejected by resources
Scalability: 50K ops/s; shard lock namespaces for more

Scaling &amp; Failure Handling
Leader failure: followers elect a new leader in ~1-2 s; sessions get a grace period on election
GC pause / partition: an expired holder may still act; fencing tokens make the resource reject it
Lost quorum: locks cannot be granted (safety over availability); existing holders keep working until TTL
Thundering herd: waiters queue FIFO and are woken one at a time
