OpenTable style

Functional Requirements
Search restaurants by location, cuisine, time, party size
Show available time slots in real time
Reserve, modify and cancel
Restaurant manages tables, hours and capacity
Waitlist when no slots available
Reminders and confirmations
No-show tracking and deposits (optional)

Non-Functional Requirements B

Correctness: never exceed table capacity per slot
Latency: slot lookup p99 < 200 ms
Availability: 99.95%
Consistency: strong per restaurant slot; eventual search
Scalability: peak at meal-time booking
Fairness: waitlist FIFO with expiry
Durability: confirmed reservations persisted

Scaling & Failure Handling R

Redis loss: slot counters rebuilt from the slots table; booking falls back to DB check with row lock
Overbooking guard: CHECK constraint in SQL is the final authority
Reminder misses: idempotent scheduled jobs; catch-up window on restart
Hot restaurants: per-restaurant rate limit and queue at opening of reservations
