Non-Functional Requirements
Latency: profile page p99 < 200 ms
Availability: 99.99% for reads
Scalability: 1B members, 100M DAU
Consistency: strong for connection accept; eventual for feeds
Graph queries: 2nd / 3rd degree connections fast
Search: typeahead < 100 ms, filterable
Privacy: visibility and blocking rules enforced

Scaling &amp; Failure Handling
Search lag: CDC via Kafka is async; profile reads always hit the DB, search can be seconds stale
Graph hot spots: members with huge degree paged and sampled for PYMK
Batch failures: PYMK lists versioned; old list served until new job succeeds
Privacy: block and visibility filters applied at read, not only at index time
