Non-Functional Requirements
Latency: critical (OTP) delivered in < 5 s
Throughput: campaign bursts of 10x average
Delivery: at-least-once with idempotent dedupe
Availability: 99.99%, channels isolated from each other
Scalability: workers scale per channel and priority
Compliance: opt-out, quiet hours, consent audit
Durability: accepted requests never lost

Key design trade-offs

At-least-once + dedupe vs exactly-once complexity

Per-channel workers (isolation) vs one generic worker

Push model (fast) vs pull for in-app inbox

Multiple providers (failover) vs cost and complexity

Scaling &amp; Failure Handling
Isolation: separate topics and consumer groups per channel and priority
Provider failure: circuit breaker plus secondary provider (e.g. SES to SendGrid)
Retries: exponential backoff via retry topics, then DLQ with alerting
Burst control: campaigns throttled to provider rate limits; invalid device tokens purged
