Web Crawler

Search-engine crawler

Functional Requirements
Crawl from seed URLs, following discovered links
Respect robots.txt and per-host rate limits
Detect duplicate URLs and duplicate content
Re-crawl pages by freshness and importance
Store raw pages and extracted content
Hand pages to the indexing pipeline
Handle traps, redirects and failures
Non-Functional Requirements
Politeness: never overload a host (1 req / few s)
Scalability: billions of URLs, horizontal workers
Robustness: survive traps, bad HTML, slow hosts
Freshness: news hourly, static pages monthly
Extensibility: pluggable parsers (PDF, images)
Efficiency: avoid re-fetching unchanged pages
Fault tolerance: resume without losing the frontier

Key design trade-offs

BFS (breadth, discovery) vs priority crawl (quality first)

Bloom filter (tiny, false positives) vs exact set (costly)

Central frontier (simple) vs per-host partitions (scalable)

Re-crawl by change rate vs fixed schedule

Scaling &amp; Failure Handling
Fetcher crash: frontier lease expires and the URLs are re-queued to another worker
Slow or dead hosts: timeouts, exponential back-off per host, temporary blacklist
Frontier durability: Kafka partitioned by host hash with replication; state recovers from Cassandra
Traps and abuse: depth, length and per-domain caps; content fingerprint catches loops
