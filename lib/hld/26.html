Non-Functional Requirements
Latency: relationship check < 10 ms, lists < 50 ms
Scalability: billions of users, hundreds of billions of edges
Consistency: follow visible to follower immediately (read-your-writes)
Availability: 99.99%
Hot spots: celebrities with 100M+ followers
Durability: edges never lost
Idempotency: duplicate follow requests safe

Async counters (cheap) vs exact counts

Bucket hot celebrity partitions vs single partition

Scaling &amp; Failure Handling
Dual-write failure: write following first, publish repair event; reconciler fixes missing mirror rows
Celebrity hot keys: bucketed partitions; follower pages served from cache
Counter drift: async counters repaired from source tables nightly
Idempotency: PUT / DELETE semantics make retries safe
