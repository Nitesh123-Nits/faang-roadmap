Inventory Management System

Stock reservation at scale

Functional Requirements
Track stock per SKU per warehouse
Reserve stock at checkout; release on cancel / timeout
Commit stock on shipment
Receive stock (inbound) and adjust counts
Real-time availability for catalog pages
Low-stock alerts and reorder triggers
Audit trail of every stock movement
Non-Functional Requirements
Correctness: never oversell (strong consistency on reserve)
Latency: reserve p99 < 30 ms
Throughput: flash-sale hot SKUs 10K+ ops/s
Availability: 99.99% for reads, reserve fails safe
Durability: every movement logged
Idempotency: same reservation id applies once
Auditability: immutable ledger
