Ride Booking System

Uber / Lyft style

Functional Requirements
Request a ride with pickup and destination
Match with a nearby available driver
Live driver location and ETA
Fare estimate with surge pricing
Trip lifecycle: requested, accepted, started, completed
Payment and receipts
Ratings, cancellations, safety features
Non-Functional Requirements
Latency: match p99 < 5 s; fare quote < 500 ms
Availability: 99.99% in each city
Real-time: driver position every 4 s
Scalability: partition by geography (city / H3 cell)
Consistency: a driver is assigned to one trip at a time
Resilience: city-level failure isolation
Accuracy: ETA within 10%

Scaling & Failure Handling R

City isolation: dispatch shards per city / cell group; one city outage does not affect others
Driver double-dispatch: atomic lock on driver; offers expire in ~10 s
Gateway loss: drivers reconnect; geo index repopulates within one ping interval
Peak demand: surge pricing balances supply; matching batches decisions during spikes
