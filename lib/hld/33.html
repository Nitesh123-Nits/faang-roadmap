Ticket Booking System

BookMyShow / Ticketmaster

Functional Requirements
Browse cities, venues, shows and seat maps
Select seats; hold them temporarily
Pay and confirm booking
Cancel and refund within policy
E-ticket with QR code
Virtual waiting room for hot events
Notifications and reminders
Non-Functional Requirements
Correctness: a seat is never sold twice
Availability: browse 99.99%; booking degrades gracefully
Latency: seat map < 200 ms; hold < 100 ms
Spikes: 100x traffic when popular tickets open
Fairness: queue admission, bot protection
Consistency: strong per seat; eventual elsewhere
Durability: confirmed bookings never lost

Waiting room (fair, extra latency) vs open stampede

Scaling &amp; Failure Handling
Flash sales: waiting room caps admitted load; seat map served from cache + small live-status call
Double sell: Redis lock for speed, DB unique constraint as the final authority
Redis failover: locks lost on failover are safe because the DB constraint rejects duplicate confirms
Payment gap: hold TTL longer than the payment timeout; late payments autorefunded
