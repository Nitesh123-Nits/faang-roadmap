Functional Requirements
Browse and search products with filters
Product detail pages with price and stock
Shopping cart that survives sessions
Checkout: address, payment, order placement
Order tracking and history
Reviews and ratings
Seller catalog management
Non-Functional Requirements
Availability: 99.99% for browse; checkout must not lose orders
Latency: product page p99 < 200 ms, search < 300 ms
Consistency: strong for stock / orders, eventual for search
Scalability: 10x spikes (sales events)
Idempotency: double-click must not double-order
Durability: orders and payments never lost
Security: PCI scope isolated to Payment Service

Redis cart (fast, lossy) vs DB cart (durable)

Scaling & Failure Handling R

Checkout failure: saga with compensations and an idempotency key per order; no half-completed orders
Sale spikes: CDN + Redis for reads; cart and browse stay up even if checkout is throttled
Search lag: CDC to Elasticsearch is async; product page always reads the DB / cache
Isolation: Payment Service in its own PCI zone; circuit breakers on PSP calls
