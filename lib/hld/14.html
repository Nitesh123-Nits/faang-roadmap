Functional Requirements
Suggest top 5-10 completions per typed prefix
Rank by popularity, recency and personalization
Update suggestions as trends change
Filter offensive and blocked terms
Support multiple languages and locales
Handle typos with fuzzy fallback
Collect queries for ranking (async)
Non-Functional Requirements
Latency: p99 < 100 ms (target < 50 ms)
Availability: 99.99%, degrade to cached results
Scalability: 10x requests per search
Freshness: trends visible within minutes to hours
Consistency: eventual, stale suggestions acceptable
Efficiency: debounce and cache at client and edge
Safety: blocklist enforced at build and serve time

Scaling & Failure Handling R

Degradation: if Trie shards fail, serve Redis or CDN cached results; client falls back to local history
Trie rollout: new snapshot loaded off-heap, then atomically swapped; keep previous version for rollback
Hot prefixes: short prefixes served from CDN; replicas per shard for read scaling
Abuse: rate limit, blocklist on build and on serve, drop low-frequency spam queries
