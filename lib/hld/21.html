WhatsApp / Messaging

1:1 and group messaging

Functional Requirements
1:1 and group chat (up to 1024 members)
Delivery states: sent, delivered, read
Offline delivery with push notifications
Media sharing (image, video, voice, documents)
Online presence and last seen
End-to-end encryption
Multi-device sync
Non-Functional Requirements
Latency: end-to-end < 100 ms when both online
Ordering: per-conversation order guaranteed
Delivery: at-least-once, client dedupe by message ID
Availability: 99.99%
Scalability: 100M+ concurrent WebSocket connections
Security: end-to-end encryption (Signal protocol)
Durability: messages stored until delivered

Scaling &amp; Failure Handling
Gateway crash: clients reconnect to another gateway and sync missed messages from Cassandra
Ordering: per-chat Kafka partition + sequence numbers; clients dedupe retries
Hot groups: large groups fan out through Kafka consumers, not inline
Offline: messages retained 30 days; push wakes the device; media via CDN
