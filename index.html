Your edge and your gaps

Read from your resume: what interviewers will dig into, and the claims to defend line by line.

✓Lead with these

  • Distributed job scheduler (Kafka consumer groups, distributed lock, retries, per-device status). Rehearse it as a 45-min design with numbers: devices, jobs/day, partitions, lag, failure modes.
  • Event-driven provisioning at Jio across thousands of devices. Use it for "a system you designed" and Ownership questions.
  • Latency work at Aditya Birla Capital: sequential → parallel async plus Redis. Know p50/p99 before and after, and how partial failures were handled.
  • Fintech context (FD, gold/silver, SIP) gives you a natural payment or ledger design to own.

!Fix before you apply

  • "Exactly-once processing semantics" with a distributed lock will be challenged. A lock stops duplicate publishing; exactly-once end to end needs idempotent consumers or Kafka transactions.
  • "JWT … improving CRUD performance by 75%" reads as cause and effect. Split what made CRUD faster from what JWT secured.
  • "Significantly cutting latency" needs a number. Write bullets as: did X, measured by Y, by doing Z.
  • LeetCode link is on your resume, so raise your contest rating before applying.
  • Skills list is long. Keep what you can whiteboard; drop AngularJS for backend roles.
